TrailRace 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-11-19

中文

TrailRace 是越野赛事信息查询与个人赛历应用。当前版本无需注册账户。用户保存的赛事、关注列表和参赛状态存储在设备本地，不由 TrailRace 运营方主动收集或上传。应用从公开的 GitHub Pages 数据地址下载赛事目录和已核实的动态。访问这些资源时，网络服务提供方可能按照其政策处理必要的技术连接信息（如 IP 地址）。

只有用户主动选择“导出备份”时，应用才通过 iOS 系统分享功能将备份交给用户选定的目标；后续处理取决于用户选择的接收方。用户可以通过应用内修改、恢复备份或卸载应用管理本地数据。卸载应用可能清除本地记录。

当前版本不提供账户、广告、应用内购买、推送通知或应用内行为分析。未来若引入这些功能，本政策将相应更新。赛事信息可能变化，请以主办方官网为准。本应用与赛事主办方不存在官方隶属关系。

English

TrailRace is a trail-race discovery and personal calendar app. The current version does not require an account. Saved races, follows and participation statuses are stored locally on your device and are not intentionally uploaded to TrailRace servers. The app downloads public race catalog and reviewed announcement data from GitHub Pages. The hosting and network providers may process technical connection data such as IP addresses under their own policies.

When you explicitly export a backup, the app invokes the iOS share sheet; any subsequent processing depends on the recipient you choose. You can modify local entries in the app or remove local data by uninstalling the app, which may erase saved records.

The current version has no accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, push notifications or in-app behavior analytics. This policy will be updated if those features are added. Race information may change; always confirm on organizers' official websites. TrailRace is independent of event organizers.

Privacy questions / 隐私问题：请通过 TrailRace 项目反馈页面联系开发者。 / Contact the developer via the project issue tracker.
